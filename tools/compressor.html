Frequently Asked Questions

What is an image compressor?

An image compressor reduces the file size of an image while attempting to maintain useful visual quality.

Does image compression reduce quality?

Depending on the compression level, image quality may decrease slightly. Higher quality settings generally preserve more visual detail.
