Why Use a Strong Password?

Strong passwords can help protect online accounts from unauthorized access. Using a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols can make passwords harder to guess.

Avoid using easily predictable information such as your name, birthday, phone number, or common words.

Strong Password Example

This is only an example. For actual accounts, generate a new password instead of reusing an example.
