How to Calculate a Percentage

Enter a number and the percentage you want to calculate. Click the Calculate button and the result will appear instantly.

The calculator uses this simple formula:

Percentage Result = (Number × Percentage) ÷ 100

Percentage Calculator Example

For example, to calculate 20% of 500:

(500 × 20) ÷ 100 = 100

So, 20% of 500 is 100.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a percentage?

A percentage represents a number as a fraction of 100. For example, 25% means 25 out of 100.

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide the result by 100.
