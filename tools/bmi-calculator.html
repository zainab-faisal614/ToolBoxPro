About BMI

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple screening measurement based on a person's height and weight. It can provide a general indication of weight status but does not directly measure body fat or overall health.

BMI should be considered alongside other health information. This calculator is for general informational purposes and is not a medical diagnosis.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is calculated using height and weight.

How is BMI calculated?

For metric measurements, BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in meters squared.

Can I use imperial measurements?

Yes. You can calculate BMI using feet, inches and pounds.
