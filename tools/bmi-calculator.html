BMI Categories

BMI Range Category
Below 18.5 Underweight
18.5 – 24.9 Normal weight
25 – 29.9 Overweight
30 or above Obesity
BMI is a general screening measure and does not provide a medical diagnosis.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a measurement calculated using a person's height and weight.

How is BMI calculated?

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in meters squared.

What is a normal BMI range?

For adults, a BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is generally classified as the normal weight range.

Is BMI a medical diagnosis?

No. BMI is a general screening measure and should not be used as a medical diagnosis by itself.

Can I calculate BMI in pounds and feet?

Yes. This calculator supports both metric (cm/kg) and imperial (ft/lbs) units.
