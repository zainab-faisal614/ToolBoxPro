Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth to calculate your exact age in years, months, and days.

How to Calculate Your Age

Select your date of birth and click the Calculate Age button. The calculator will calculate your exact age based on today's date.

Your result is displayed in years, months, and days.

Why Use an Age Calculator?

An age calculator makes it easy to find your exact age quickly without doing manual date calculations.

It can also be useful when checking your age for forms, applications, birthdays, or other everyday purposes.

Age Calculator Example

Enter your date of birth, for example January 1, 2000, and click "Calculate Age".

The calculator will show your current age in years, months, and days.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the age calculator work?

Enter your date of birth and the calculator compares it with today's date to calculate your age in years, months, and days.

Is this age calculator free?

Yes. ToolBoxPro's age calculator is completely free to use and does not require an account.

Can I calculate my exact age?

Yes. The calculator provides your age in years, months, and days based on your selected date of birth.

Does the calculator store my date of birth?

No. The calculation is performed directly in your browser.
