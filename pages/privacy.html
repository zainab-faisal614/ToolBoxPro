Privacy Policy

ToolBoxPro respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our free online tools and website.

Information We Collect

ToolBoxPro does not currently require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the basic online tools.

Images and Files

The Image Compressor processes selected images directly in your web browser for its basic functionality. The image does not need to be uploaded to a ToolBoxPro server for compression.

Information You Enter

Some tools allow you to enter information such as text, URLs or other content. This information is used by the selected tool to provide its requested functionality.

As a general precaution, avoid entering passwords, financial information or other highly sensitive information into online tools.

Cookies and Analytics

ToolBoxPro does not currently use its own analytics or advertising cookies as part of the basic website functionality.

If analytics, advertising or other services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

Third-Party Services

Some website features may use third-party libraries or services. These third parties may have their own privacy policies and terms that apply to their services.

External Links

ToolBoxPro may link to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ToolBoxPro adds new features or services. Any changes will be published on this page.
